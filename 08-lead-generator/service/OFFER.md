# Qualified B2B Lead Generation Service

Get clean, verified, and campaign-ready B2B prospect lists for direct outbound sales (cold email, cold calling, or WhatsApp), without wasting dozens of hours manually browsing business directories or burning your domain reputation with stale data.

---

## 1. What You Receive in Every Delivery

A turnkey delivery file in **Excel (`.xlsx`)** and **CSV** format, structured across four dedicated worksheets ready for immediate sales execution:

1. **Leads:** Comprehensive prospect table featuring enriched business data:
   - **Company Name:** Clean legal or trade name (suffixes stripped and standardized).
   - **Category:** Specific industry or vertical.
   - **Normalized Phone:** International E.164 standard (e.g. `+573001234567`), ready for one-click dialers or CRMs.
   - **Verified Corporate Email:** Checked for valid syntax and active MX DNS records.
   - **Website:** Clean URL without tracking parameters.
   - **Physical Address & City:** Local geographic targeting data.
   - **Quality Score (0–100):** Algorithmic rating rewarding completeness and contactability, styled with a visual conditional color scale.
   - **Source:** Verified public origin of the record.
2. **Summary ("Resumen"):** Pivot metrics breaking down totals by industry category and score distribution (High, Medium, Low).
3. **Sources ("Fuentes"):** Audit log showing data sources queried, leads extracted, and deduplicated records merged.
4. **Tracking ("Seguimiento"):** Built-in mini-CRM worksheet to track campaign pipeline: lead status, contact date, prospect response, and next steps.

## 3. What Is NOT Included

To establish clear expectations and protect your brand:

- **Does NOT include sending emails or making outbound calls:** We deliver clean, enriched, and audited data; executing the sales campaign remains with your sales team (or can be quoted as a separate add-on).
- **Does NOT include private consumer data (B2C):** We strictly extract public commercial data from businesses (B2B). We do not collect or sell private personal residential records.
- **Does NOT include black-hat scraping or anti-bot bypass:** We do not crack protected portals, bypass CAPTCHAs, or violate public terms of service. All extraction is polite, compliant with `robots.txt`, and rate-limited.
- **Does NOT guarantee closed sales or conversion rates:** We guarantee data accuracy, low bounce rates, and niche relevance; closing deals depends on your product, offer, and sales team follow-up.
- **Does NOT include third-party paid API quotas:** If custom paid sources are requested (such as official Google Places API keys for mass volume), the client provides their own API key.
